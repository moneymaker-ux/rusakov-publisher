Rusakov Publisher

An internal tool that publishes and manages videos on the YouTube channel of Pavel Rusakov, an independent creator. It is used only by the channel owner and is not available to the public.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's own videos. Finished Shorts are uploaded from the owner's computer with a title, description, privacy status and scheduled publish time. A video is uploaded only after the owner approves it in the content queue.
  • Reads statistics of the channel's own videos (views, likes, comments) so the owner can plan new content. In development.
  • Helps answer comments. Comments on the channel's own videos are collected, a reply draft is prepared, and the reply is posted only after the owner reviews and approves it. In development.

How it works

The tool runs on the owner's computer. It signs in to YouTube with Google OAuth 2.0, using the channel owner's own Google account, and calls the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. Nobody else can sign in; there is no sign-up.
